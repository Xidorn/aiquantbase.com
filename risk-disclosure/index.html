Risk disclosure

The scope and limits of educational material, examples and calculators.

Educational scope

Content explains methods and tools. It is not individualized investment advice, a recommendation to trade a security or a promise of performance.

Historical tests and models

Backtests depend on data and execution assumptions. They can be affected by bias, selection and omitted costs. Models and AI-generated explanations may contain errors. Historical and simulated outcomes do not guarantee future outcomes.

Calculators and execution

Tools apply simplified mathematical models to your inputs. They do not connect to a broker, estimate live liquidity or execute trades. Stop-based position calculations do not guarantee a maximum loss because actual fills may differ from the input price.
