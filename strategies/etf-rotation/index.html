ETF rotation: a reproducible specification

ETF rotation changes allocations among a defined set of funds at scheduled decision times. Universe selection and rebalance timing are central to the specification.

AI Quant Base editorialPublished 2026-10-26Updated 2026-10-26

Fix the universe and ranking

State which funds are eligible, when they entered the universe and how returns are calculated. Define a lookback window, ranking direction, number of selections and tie-breaking rule. Using today’s available funds throughout history can distort the test.

Model the rebalance

Specify a calendar, signal observation time and executable trade time. Include transaction costs and rules for assets without sufficient history. State whether weights are equal, fixed or derived from another calculation. Cash handling must also be explicit.

Keep comparisons consistent

Compare versions over a common date range with compatible return and cost conventions. Report changes in holdings and turnover alongside aggregate performance. This page describes a research design and does not supply a ranked list of funds to buy.
