Define a financial research brief

Use a concise brief to turn a broad trading idea into a researchable question. It can support your own work or a conversation with a research collaborator.

AI Quant Base editorialPublished 2026-10-26Updated 2026-10-26

Objective and decision

Name one question: understand a signal, test a ranking rule, compare allocation rules, or review historical risk. State what evidence would help answer it. “Find a profitable strategy” is not a sufficiently defined experiment.

Scope and available information

Record asset type, intended venue, observation frequency, currency, date range and available data. List missing information separately. This site does not supply live market data, so data availability must be established in your research environment.

Constraints and evaluation

Specify long-only or other exposure, position limits, rebalance timing, execution assumptions and costs. Define benchmark and hold-out method before reading results. Constraints describe the experiment; they are not promises that losses stay within a chosen level.

A copyable brief

Research question: __; asset type and venue: __; frequency and period: __; data source: __; rule: __; constraints: __; costs: __; benchmark: __; validation: __; expected output: __; unresolved questions: __. Do not include passwords, account numbers or trading keys.

Start the practical task

Use the research studio to document rules or inspect a suitable equity series. Review the input assumptions and export your work before leaving.
