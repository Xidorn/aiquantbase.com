Research applications

Review a strategy research report

For research collaborators who need a shared review standard. Trace every conclusion back to data, rules and a reproducible calculation.

AI Quant Base editorialPublished 2026-10-26Updated 2026-10-26

Request a complete handoff

Request the research question, data provenance and version, configuration, code revision, decision timestamps, cost assumptions and output files. Separate an illustrative demonstration from an actual historical experiment.

Review in a fixed order

Check information timing first; inspect a sample of signals and fills; reconcile totals; then examine held-out evaluation and sensitivity. If inputs or timing are unresolved, record the result as not yet verified rather than inferring quality from a chart.

Keep a decision log

For each issue, record the question, evidence location, reviewer, status and required follow-up. Use statuses such as open, reproduced or resolved with a stated limitation. These are review states, not investment ratings.

Define a useful deliverable

Produce a brief that distinguishes confirmed calculations, assumptions and unresolved evidence. AI Quant Base offers public guides, local equity analysis and calculators for this process; no audit certification, managed workspace or institutional client engagement is implied.

Start the practical task

Use the research studio to document rules or inspect a suitable equity series. Review the input assumptions and export your work before leaving.
