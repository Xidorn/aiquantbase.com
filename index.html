BEFORE YOU START

Know what to expect.

What can I do here today?

Create and export a structured strategy brief, import a dated equity series, inspect total change and drawdown, and use five standalone calculators. Each tool explains its inputs and calculation scope.

Do I need an account or a data connection?

No account or external data account is required. Inputs are processed in the current browser tab. Export your work before leaving, because edits are not saved automatically.

Can the studio run AI models or backtests?

The current version provides structured editing and deterministic analysis. It does not call an AI model, fetch market prices or execute a strategy. The research library explains how to review these workflows in your own environment.

How can I check an exported result?

Read the formula and measurement basis, compare the original observations and reimport the JSON report. Equity metrics are recalculated on import. The report establishes arithmetic consistency with the inputs; it does not verify the data source.
