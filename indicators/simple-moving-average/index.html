Simple moving average: calculation and timing

A simple moving average is the arithmetic mean of a fixed number of observations. The input field and window determine what it measures.

AI Quant Base editorialPublished 2026-10-26Updated 2026-10-26

Compute a rolling mean

For n observations, SMA = sum of the last n inputs divided by n. An illustrative sequence 10, 12, 14 has a three-observation SMA of 12. These are constructed values, not market data.

Define readiness

A full-window calculation requires n observations. Specify how the strategy behaves during warm-up and whether current-bar information is complete. An average of closing prices should not be treated as known before that close.

Separate indicator and strategy

An SMA summarizes past observations. A strategy still needs entry, exit, sizing and execution rules. Smoothing changes the response to new inputs; it does not establish predictive value by itself.
