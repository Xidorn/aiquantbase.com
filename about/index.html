About AI Quant Base

AI Quant Base is operated by Mr Wang and provides public guides and calculation tools for AI-assisted quantitative research.

Website operator

AI Quant Base is operated by Mr Wang. The website brings together educational guides, research methods and practical calculation tools for readers exploring AI-assisted quantitative research.

What this website covers

AI Quant Base organizes educational material around AI quant research, trading strategy rules, historical testing, indicators and calculation methods. The purpose is to make methods, assumptions and sources easier to inspect.

What is available

The website includes a strategy brief builder, CSV equity analysis with report export, public guides and five browser-based calculators. It does not offer brokerage accounts, order execution or an online strategy backtesting service.

How to use the material

Start with a question, read the relevant method, inspect the assumptions and follow the original references where needed. Send factual corrections or suggestions to the contact address.
