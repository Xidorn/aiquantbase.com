Transaction costs and slippage in a backtest

Transaction costs reduce the difference between gross and net outcomes. A useful model states which fees and execution effects it includes.

AI Quant Base editorialPublished 2026-10-26Updated 2026-10-26

Separate the components

List commissions, exchange or regulatory fees where applicable, spreads and slippage. Borrowing or financing costs require separate treatment when relevant. A single flat number may be a useful approximation, but label the approximation.

Apply costs to transactions

A buy and a later sell are two transaction legs. In a simplified notional model, round-trip cost is buy notional times its cost rate plus sell notional times its cost rate. Different prices can produce different notionals.

Check sensitivity

Re-run the same rules with more conservative cost assumptions and inspect changes in turnover-heavy periods. Slippage is not constant across orders or markets. The calculator on this site uses your inputs and does not estimate live liquidity.
