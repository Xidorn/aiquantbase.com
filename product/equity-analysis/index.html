Equity analysis & drawdown reports

Analyze a dated equity series with visible calculation rules and an exportable record of the observations.

Analyze my data ↗Read the data format ↗

Bring your own observations

Use a two-column date,equity CSV with 2–5,000 positive observations in chronological order. The tool checks valid dates, repeated dates and numeric values before calculation.

Read four useful measures

Total change, CAGR on a 365-day basis, sampled maximum drawdown and observation count are calculated from your sequence. Charts use the actual observation dates.

Clarify cash flows and costs

Confirm no external deposits or withdrawals, or use an already cash-flow-adjusted series. Costs are labeled according to your declaration; the tool does not estimate or deduct extra fees.

A report you can reopen

Export a Markdown review or JSON with the original observations and calculation version. Reimporting JSON recalculates the metrics, instead of trusting numbers stored in the file.

An equity path cannot establish trade-level win rate, explain every exposure or reconstruct unobserved losses. This version does not calculate Sharpe ratios or infer individual trades.
