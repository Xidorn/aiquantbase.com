For the work before and after a backtest

Specify assumptions before implementing a strategy. After a test in your own research environment, import a suitable equity series and review the resulting path. The two tools are independent: a strategy brief does not automatically create equity observations.

AI research with an explicit scope

The current tools use structured editing and deterministic calculations. The AI research library explains model-assisted workflows; this version has no connected language model, market feed, backtest engine or broker integration.

No account is required. Your input is processed in the current browser tab; export it before closing or refreshing the page.
