Maximum drawdown: formula and interpretation

Maximum drawdown measures the largest observed percentage decline from a running equity peak to a subsequent trough in a chosen series.

AI Quant Base editorialPublished 2026-10-26Updated 2026-10-26

Calculate from a running peak

For positive equity E at time t, let P be the largest equity observed up to t. Drawdown magnitude is 1 − E/P. Maximum drawdown is the largest such magnitude in the series. Some reports show drawdown as a negative return; state the sign convention.

A constructed example

In an illustrative series 100, 120, 90, 110, the peak is 120 and the subsequent trough is 90. The maximum decline is 25%. These numbers are constructed to explain arithmetic and are not strategy returns.

Frequency changes what is observed

End-of-day values can miss intraday declines. Deposits and withdrawals can also distort raw account equity; use a consistently defined return series when appropriate. Read recovery duration and the full path alongside the maximum.
