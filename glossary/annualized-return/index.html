Annualized return and CAGR

CAGR converts the ratio between positive starting and ending values into a constant annual compound rate over a stated holding period.

AI Quant Base editorialPublished 2026-10-26Updated 2026-10-26

State the formula and period

For starting value S, ending value F and holding period y years, CAGR = (F/S)^(1/y) − 1. The year-length convention should be explicit. This site’s calculator uses user-entered days divided by 365.

A constructed example

A value moving from 100 to 121 over two years has a 10% compound annual rate. This is a mathematical example, not an investment forecast. A short-period annualization can magnify a temporary change.

Know when endpoints are insufficient

The endpoint formula does not correctly isolate investment return when external cash flows occur during the period. Use an appropriate time-weighted or money-weighted method instead. CAGR also does not show the size or timing of interim losses.
